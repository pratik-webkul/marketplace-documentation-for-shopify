# User Guide for Shipentegra Shipping

The [**Multi-vendor Marketplace**](/zenith/introduction/) has introduced another feature app for order fulfillment named Shipentegra Shipping feature app.

With this **Integration**, the admin can connect multiple carriers, automate order fulfillment, and streamline shipping workflows.

It helps improve delivery accuracy and scalability while simplifying shipping management for growing marketplaces.

Shipentegra ships your products worldwide with multiple services and competitive prices using its marketplace shipping integration.

To enable the feature in the app, the admin can allow sellers to manage order fulfillment using Shipentegra and generate shipping labels for their orders.

This feature app will charge you **$10 per month** in addition to your current Multivendor plan.

THIS SHIPPING FEATURE APP IS USED **ONLY FOR FULFILLMENT**.

How to Enable Feature App?
--------------------------

To enable the feature app, simply visit the “**Featured Apps**” section of the admin panel and enable it from there.

![Shipentegra Marketplace Shipping Integration](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/sipentraone-1200x837.webp)

Once you click on **“Feature Apps”**, you will see the list of apps available in that section. From there, locate the feature app and simply click the **Activate** button.

![Shipentegra Marketplace Shipping Integration](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/shipentratwo-1200x837.webp)

Once you click on **“Activate”**, you will be prompted to approve the charge for this feature app.

![Shipentegra Marketplace Shipping Integration](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/shipentrathree-1200x837.webp)

 **Workflow**
--------------
ADMIN END
------------


Once the feature app is enabled, a new sub-menu named **Shipentegra Shipping Configuration** will be added under the app’s **Configuration** menu.

![Shipentegra Marketplace Shipping Integration](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/shipentraconfig-1164x1024.webp)

**Note:** You must have an active account to configure the details in the app.

Before proceeding, please navigate to **Configuration >> Shipping Methods** and enable **Shipentegra Shipping**, as shown in the screenshot below.

![Shipentegra Marketplace Shipping Integration](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/sipentrashippingmethod-1200x1018.webp)

Now, proceed to configure, enter the required details such as the **Shipentegra Client ID** and **Secret Key**, and then save the configuration.

![Shipentegra Marketplace Shipping Integration](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/shipentracongig-1200x479.webp)

To obtain the **Client ID** and **Secret Key**, you need to configure them from your **Shipentegra Shipping account**.

In the configuration, you can choose whether the Shipentegra label cost will be borne by the **admin** or the **seller**.

![Shipentegra](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/configshipntra-1200x510.webp)

 Seller Panel Configuration
--------------------
Once these details are configured, sellers will also have the option to enable **Shipentegra Shipping** from the **Shipping Method Configuration** under the **Configuration** menu in their seller panel.

![Shipentegra Marketplace Shipping Integration](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/shipentrashipmeth-1200x811.webp)

![Shipentegra Marketplace Shipping Integration](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/shipentrashipmethod-1200x811.webp)

Once the seller enables the method, its status will appear as **Enabled**, and they will be able to fulfill their orders by selecting **Shipentegra Shipping**.

 Order Fulfillment
-----------------
The seller can fulfill an order by logging in to the **Multi-vendor Seller Panel**, navigating to **Orders >> Order Listing**, and selecting the order they want to fulfill.

Click on **View** to open the order details.

![Shipentegra Marketplace Shipping Integration](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/vieworder-1200x533.webp)

Then, click on **Proceed to Fulfill** to complete the fulfillment.

![Shipentegra Marketplace Shipping Integration](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/prdfullfill-1200x874.webp)

![fulfill now](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/fulfillnow-1200x874.webp)

Select **Shipentegra Shipping** as the fulfillment method.

![fulfill](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/shippingselect-1200x536.webp)

Once you select, the available shipping services and rates will be displayed.

You can then choose the required shipping service based on the rates, click on **“Agree to deduct from earnings”**, and finally click **“Agree and Fulfill.”**

![fulfill](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/rateagree-1200x965.webp)

Once you click **“Agree and Fulfill”**, the product will be successfully fulfilled.

The tracking details, such as the **tracking number**, **tracking URL**, and **shipping label** will be generated automatically for fulfilled orders.

You can easily generate and print the shipping label by clicking on the **Action** button.

![track](https://cdnblog.webkul.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/trckdetails-1200x965.webp)
