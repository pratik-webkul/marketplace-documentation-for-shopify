# User Guide for Lable Translations

Let's learn about Label Translation in Multivendor Marketplace for Shopify.

Multivendor Marketplace App is an ultimate tool to convert your store into a fully-functioning marketplace.

Here, multiple sellers get registered who can sell their products locally or globally and admin can charge a commission on each seller’s earning.

In [Multivendor Marketplace for Shopify](/zenith/introduction/), as per your requirement, now you can rename labels of various sections of the app. Let's have a look at how it works.

So now you can get the labels changed as per your requirement and also in your choice of language.
